# Stage 0 correction scope and postpartum boundaries

Decision date: 10 September 2026. Owner: Codex implementation under Aswath's request
to address the supplied correction plan. Product/clinical acceptance is pending.

Retain all 63 addresses and the original nine representative profiles. Populate
all eight early postpartum day overlays as review drafts, with general recovery
support repeated honestly and scheduled-contact cards only on their supported days.
Other weekly shells remain hidden with explicit blockers. P42 stays unpublished.

Postpartum day 0 is the date of delivery. PP01 includes days 0 through 7; PP02
includes days 8 through 14; PP06 includes days 36 through 42. Day 7 therefore adds
PPD7 to PP01. This is a named product grouping, not an inferred clinical schedule.
Day and week evidence remain separate records; scope checks never convert them
implicitly. The day-to-week helper implements this grouping explicitly.

P01 is a pregnancy-timing explanation. It must not require nutrition, exercise or
birth preparation merely to pass a generic completeness checklist. Exact-week
dating is not proof that an individual is pregnant or that conception occurred.

The correction pass also creates the broader foundation contracts requested in
the review: food, movement, wellbeing, follow-up and comparison catalogues,
conditions, safety rules, plan schema, eight fictional documents with extraction
truth and a frozen offline evaluation contract. This does not implement specialist
agents, OCR, a database, hosted LangSmith execution or the complete application.

Clinical, content, licence, India-localisation and product approvals must bind to
the current release fingerprint. An agent assessment is labelled as such and
cannot satisfy a human review role. The review document's extra approval roles
are now an explicit release requirement; they were not previously all implemented.

Fruit/object comparisons from the review are editorial proposals. Unverified
measurements and unmeasured objects stay hidden. Illustrations are explicitly
deferred by section 12.4 of the supplied review. We will not invent measurements
or label a hidden comparison as reviewed to claim completion.
